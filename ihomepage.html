Can I try a session before joining?

Absolutely. Your first session is on us. Drop by the front desk or get in touch with our team to arrange your visit.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

Not at all. We welcome every fitness level, and our coaches can help you find a pace and program that feels right for you.

What should I bring for my first workout?

Comfortable workout clothes, supportive shoes, and a water bottle are all you need. We’ll help with the rest.
